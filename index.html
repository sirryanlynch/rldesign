+ The story behind it

The gap

Facilities teams juggled work orders, licensing, and vendors across spreadsheets and phone calls. Status was always stale, and dispatching the right technician meant guesswork.

The move

One platform to raise a work order, match it to the right service line and licensed provider, and track it to done, with every job's status easy to see.

The system

Rebuilt on a tightened component library so the team could ship new modules like licenses, service lines, and dispatch in hours, not days.

Where it landed

A clearer path from request to resolution, and a design language the product kept extending long after launch.
